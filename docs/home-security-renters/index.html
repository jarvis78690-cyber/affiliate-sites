Renter Security Buying Guide

Three Rules for Renters

  • No Permanent Installation: No drilling, no wiring, nothing that needs a landlord permit.
  • Wireless Everything: Battery-powered or plug-in. No running cables through walls.
  • Take It With You: When you move, the system leaves with you—no loss to security deposit.

Doorbell vs Cameras

  • Doorbell Camera: Entry point protection. See who's at your door 24/7. Catches package thieves.
  • Interior Cameras: Monitor living room, bedroom, valuables. Deters break-ins.
  • Combo: Doorbell + 1-2 interior cameras gives full coverage without overkill.

Cloud Storage vs Local

  • Cloud: Video stored on company servers. Accessible from anywhere. Privacy concerns. Usually $5-10/month after free trial.
  • Local: Video stored on device or homebase. No cloud dependency. Better privacy. Can't access remotely.

Battery Life Matters

  • Check battery specs. Some cameras last 2 years, others 2 months.
  • Battery + plug-in options are ideal (you choose based on location).

FAQs

Can my landlord see my security cameras?

No. Your cloud account is private. But check your lease—some landlords have rules about cameras facing shared spaces (hallways). Interior-only setups avoid conflicts.

Do I need WiFi for wireless cameras?

Yes, most wireless cameras need WiFi to send alerts and store video in cloud. Exception: eufy (local storage). If your WiFi is weak, consider mobile hotspot backup.

Will cameras hurt my security deposit?

Wireless systems leave no marks—zero damage. You're fine. Hardwired systems might require landlord approval and damage repair.

Is cloud storage safe?

Major brands (Google, Amazon, eufy) encrypt video. Yes, it's safe. If privacy is a concern, choose local-only storage (eufy).
