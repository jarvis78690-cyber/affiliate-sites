Power Bank Buyer's Guide

Understanding mAh

mAh = milliamp-hours (battery capacity). Higher = more charges. iPhone 13 battery = 3,240 mAh. A 20,000 mAh bank charges it ~6 times. But real-world efficiency = 70-80%, so expect 5-6 charges.

Fast Charging Matters

  • Standard (5W): Slow but safe
  • Fast (18W+): Charges faster, uses modern USB-C
  • Ultra-fast (30W+): Premium option, newest phones support it

Ports: USB-A vs USB-C

  • USB-A: Older standard, works with most cables. Being phased out.
  • USB-C: Modern standard, faster, reversible. Future-proof.
  • Multiple ports: Charge 2-3 devices at once.

Weight Considerations

  • <5 oz = pocket-sized, sacrifice capacity
  • 5-8 oz = balanced sweet spot
  • >10 oz = heavy but massive capacity for trips

FAQs

Will a power bank damage my battery?

No. Quality power banks have protection circuits. Your phone only charges at the speed it accepts. Anker, Belkin, and Ravpower are safe brands.

How long do power banks last?

3-5 years of heavy use. After that, capacity degrades (normal for all batteries). Replace every 5 years for optimal performance.

Can I bring a power bank on a plane?

Yes. TSA allows power banks up to 100Wh (~27,000 mAh). Most under-$50 models are compliant. Check your specific model.
