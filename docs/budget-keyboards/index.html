Buying Guide

What to Look For

  • Switch Type: Mechanical switches come in three main types: linear (smooth), tactile (bump), and clicky (audible). Gaming prefers linear; typing prefers tactile.
  • Layout: 60% = ultra-compact, 75% = ideal balance, 88-104% = full-featured.
  • Hot-Swap: Allows switch replacement without soldering. Essential for customization.
  • Keycap Quality: Double-shot keycaps last longer than printed. Check ABS vs PBT plastic.
  • Stabilizers: Smooth stabilizers for spacebar and shift = better overall feel.

FAQs

Are mechanical keyboards worth the money?

Yes. Mechanical keyboards last 5-10 years vs 2-3 for rubber dome. Better typing feel, individual switch customization, and they hold value. Cost per year is competitive.

What's the difference between Cherry MX and Outemu?

Cherry MX is the gold standard, smoother and more consistent. Outemu is budget-friendly, works great for gaming and typing, but not quite as refined. For under $100, Outemu is a smart trade-off.

Can I replace switches later?

Only if your keyboard is hot-swap compatible. Most boards under $100 now support this. Non-hot-swap boards require desoldering, which is complex.
