Buying Guide

Electric vs Manual

  • Electric: Convenient, one-click preset heights, smooth, fast. Worth the extra $100.
  • Manual: Reliable, no motor failures, lower price, durable. OK with hand-cranking every 1-2 hours.

Height Range & Reach

  • Most humans fit 28"-48" range. If you're very tall (6'4"+) or short (under 5'), check specs carefully.
  • 29"-47" is the sweet spot for average users.

Stability Matters

  • Dual motors = less wobble. Look for reviews mentioning stability.
  • Weight capacity should exceed your monitor + keyboard + mouse + lamp weight by 20%.

Desktop Quality

  • Particle board warps and sags. Real wood (bamboo, oak) lasts 10+ years.
  • Under $300, check what the desktop is made of.

Health Benefits

  • Calorie Burn: Standing burns 50 extra calories/hour vs sitting. Over a year, that's ~5 lbs.

FAQs

How often should I switch between sitting and standing?

Ideal: 30 minutes sitting, 30 minutes standing. If that's too frequent, try 1 hour sitting, 45 minutes standing. The key is movement—not staying in one position all day.

Will a standing desk fix my back pain?

Maybe. Standing desks help, but they're not a cure. Proper posture, core strength, and ergonomic setup (monitor height, keyboard position) matter more than standing alone.

Is electric or manual better?

Electric is more convenient. Manual is more reliable. For under $300, electric gives you more features. If you want simplicity and durability, manual is solid.
