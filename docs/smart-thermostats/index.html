Smart Thermostat Buying Guide

  • Learning Thermostats: AI learns your patterns and adjusts automatically. Saves more energy. Google Nest, Ecobee.

Installation: DIY vs Professional

FAQs

Can I install a smart thermostat myself?

Most can. Turn off power, note old thermostat wiring, connect new one (usually just 4 wires). Takes 20-30 minutes. If unsure, hire a pro ($100-150).

Will my HVAC system work with a smart thermostat?

Most modern systems yes. Check compatibility before buying. Older systems might need professional installation or won't work. Google Nest and Ecobee are most compatible.

How much money will I save?

$10-15/month average = $120-180/year. Varies by climate, current thermostat, and usage patterns. Learning thermostats save more than basic ones.

What if my WiFi goes down?

Thermostat still works on its last programmed schedule. You just can't control it remotely. Local control still works fine.
