Laptop Stand Buying Guide

Freestanding vs Mounted

  • Freestanding: No installation, portable, simple. Best for most users.
  • Mounted (arm/clamp): Fixed to desk, takes up no desk space, more expensive. For permanent desks.

Portability Matters

  • Home Office Only: Heavy aluminum stand OK (Nulaxy, Lamicall)
  • Work from Coffee Shops: Lightweight (Roost, Nulaxy)
  • Frequent Travel: Ultra-compact, rolls up (Roost V3)

Adjustment vs Fixed Height

  • Adjustable: Better for varying desk heights, monitor combo setups. Recommended.
  • Fixed height: Cheaper, simpler. OK if your setup is permanent.

Material Quality

  • Aluminum: Lightweight, durable, feels premium. Most popular.
  • Steel: Heavier, more stable, rusts without coating. Avoid unless plated.
  • Plastic: Budget option, can wobble. Only if under $15.

FAQs

What's the ideal screen height?

Top of screen at eye level when you look straight ahead. Laptop on stand + external keyboard achieves this. Prevents downward neck strain.

Do I need a keyboard if I use a stand?

Yes. Stand alone just elevates screen. If you use laptop keyboard while elevated, you'll strain your wrists. External keyboard + stand = proper ergonomics.

Will a stand fit my 17" laptop?

Most stands support 10"-17". Check the product specs. Some max out at 15". Verify before buying if you have a large laptop.
