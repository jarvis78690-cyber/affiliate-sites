Keyboard Buying Guide

Switch Types Explained:

Mechanical Switches: Durable, tactile, perfect for gaming and typing. Best for serious users.
Membrane Switches: Quieter, more affordable, ideal for casual gaming.
Hot-Swappable: Easy to customize and replace switches without soldering.
